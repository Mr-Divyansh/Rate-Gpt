# Rate GPT — Product Definition

## One-line description
Rate GPT helps users find reported agricultural commodity prices across Indian mandis through a simple searchable price board.

## Product promise
Find mandi price information without navigating a complicated data portal.

## Audience
Primary: farmers and small agricultural traders. Secondary: students and market researchers.

## Core value
- Easier discovery of market price records.
- Location and commodity filters.
- Clear min, max and modal prices.
- Historical context when sufficient data has been collected.

## MVP
1. Mandi price board.
2. State → district → market filters.
3. Commodity search.
4. Price display with arrival date.
5. Pagination.
6. Source attribution and last-sync time.
7. Light and dark themes.

## Product boundaries
Rate GPT reports source data. It does not guarantee transaction prices or provide price predictions.

## Success measures
Successful syncs, valid-record rate, filter/search response time, successful price discovery and data freshness.
