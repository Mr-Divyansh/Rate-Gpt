01

The purpose

Why Rate-GPT exists

Mandi prices are reported across many markets and dates, which makes them hard to compare. Rate-GPT brings those records into one searchable view for any crop, state, district and mandi.

The goal is practical price discovery. Rate-GPT doesn’t predict what a crop will sell for or replace your own judgement. Use the records alongside crop quality, transport costs and local demand.

Where the numbers come from

Data source and transparency

The intended source is the Government of India Open Government Data platform (AGMARKNET). Rate-GPT stores records from successful syncs and shows the values it holds.

Freshness depends on the source and on successful syncs. A sync time is not an arrival date, so use the date on each record. Missing values show as unavailable and are never estimated.

Visit data.gov.in

Use the data thoughtfully

Limits to keep in mind

  • Prices are reported for one crop, market and arrival date. They may not match a current offer.
  • Not every mandi, crop or date has data.
  • Rate-GPT doesn’t guarantee a sale price and doesn’t account for quality, fees, transport or negotiation.
  • Confirm current rates with the mandi before you decide.
