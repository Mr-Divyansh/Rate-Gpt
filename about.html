Explore mandi prices

Market prices are observations, not promises. Check the arrival date and confirm details at the mandi.

Why Rate-GPT exists

Mandi prices are reported across many markets and dates, which makes them hard to compare. Rate-GPT brings those records into one searchable view for any crop, state, district and mandi.

The goal is practical price discovery. Rate-GPT doesn’t predict what a crop will sell for or replace your own judgement. Use the records alongside crop quality, transport costs and local demand.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose a location

    Pick a state and district, then a mandi from the list.

  2. 2

    Find a crop

    Search the mandi’s stored records and sort them by date or price.

  3. 3

    Compare the rates

    Read the minimum, maximum and modal price with the arrival date. Prices are in ₹ per quintal.

Data source and transparency

The intended source is the Government of India Open Government Data platform (AGMARKNET). Rate-GPT stores records from successful syncs and shows the values it holds.

Freshness depends on the source and on successful syncs. A sync time is not an arrival date, so use the date on each record. Missing values show as unavailable and are never estimated.

Visit data.gov.in

Limits to keep in mind

  • Prices are reported for one crop, market and arrival date. They may not match a current offer.
  • Not every mandi, crop or date has data.
  • Rate-GPT doesn’t guarantee a sale price and doesn’t account for quality, fees, transport or negotiation.
  • Confirm current rates with the mandi before you decide.

About Rate-GPT

Rate-GPT is an agricultural market intelligence platform created to help farmers access and understand mandi price information more easily. The platform allows users to explore crop prices, compare minimum, maximum and modal rates, check market arrival dates, and discover agricultural market information. Our goal is to make agricultural market information easier to access, understand and use so that farmers can make more informed selling decisions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make agricultural market information more accessible through technology and provide farmers with useful information for better market decisions.

How Rate-GPT Works

  1. 1

    Crop-based mandi price discovery

    Search and explore mandi prices by selecting a crop, state, district, and mandi to find recorded prices for your produce.

  2. 2

    Minimum price information

    View the minimum price recorded for a crop at a specific mandi, providing the floor rate for the produce in that market.

  3. 3

    Maximum price information

    View the maximum price recorded for a crop at a specific mandi, providing the highest rate observed in that market.

  4. 4

    Modal price information

    View the modal (most frequent) price for a crop at a specific mandi, representing the most common price range for the produce.

  5. 5

    Market arrival dates

    Check the market arrival date for each price record, showing when the crop arrived at the mandi, helping you plan around recent arrivals.

  6. 6

    Mandi and market comparisons

    Compare prices across different mandis and markets for the same crop, helping you identify the best selling opportunities based on recorded data.
