WHY RATE-GPT EXISTS

Useful market information should be easier to find and understand.

Mandi prices are reported across different markets and dates. Rate-GPT is designed to bring those records into a searchable view, helping visitors find observations for a crop, state, district and mandi.

The aim is practical price discovery—not to predict what a crop will sell for or replace a farmer’s judgement. Use the records as one input alongside crop quality, transport costs, local demand and current market advice.

DATA SOURCE & TRANSPARENCY

Records are served by the Rate-GPT backend.

The project identifies the Government of India Open Government Data (OGD) platform and AGMARKNET resource as its intended upstream source. The backend stores records from successful synchronizations and the website displays the values it returns.

Availability and freshness depend on the upstream source and successful syncs. A sync time is not the same as a market arrival date; use the date shown with each price record. Missing values are shown as unavailable rather than estimated.

A NOTE ON LIMITATIONS

Use records as a guide, not a guarantee.

  • Prices reflect reported records for a specific crop, market and arrival date; they may not match a current offer.
  • Not every mandi, crop or date will have data available.
  • Rate-GPT does not guarantee a sale price and does not account for quality, fees, transport or negotiation.
  • Confirm current rates and trading conditions with the relevant mandi before making a decision.
