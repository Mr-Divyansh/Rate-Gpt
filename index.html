How to read a mandi rate

Each record gives three prices for one crop on one arrival date. Start with the modal price.

Before you sell

  • Check the arrival date. An old record may not match today’s market.
  • Compare two or three nearby mandis, then subtract transport costs.
  • Quality and moisture in your crop change the price you get.

Where these numbers come from

Rate-GPT stores prices published on the Government of India’s open data platform (AGMARKNET) and shows them as reported. It doesn’t fill in missing values or predict future prices.
