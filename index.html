A practical guide for farmers

Understand mandi prices before comparing markets

Rate-GPT helps you explore reported market observations by crop and location. Use the record date, market name and price range together; a single number never tells the whole story.

What is the minimum price?

The minimum price is the lowest price reported for a crop in a particular market record. It helps show the lower end of the recorded price range, but it does not guarantee the price a farmer will receive.

What is the maximum price?

The maximum price is the highest price reported in that market record. It may relate to produce with different quality or trading conditions, so it should not be treated as the expected price for every lot.

Why check the modal price?

The modal price is the most frequently reported price for the crop in that market record. It can provide a useful reference alongside the minimum, maximum, arrival date and local market conditions.

How to use Rate-GPT responsibly

Select a state, district and mandi, then search for a crop. Compare records from similar dates and check the unit before comparing prices. Consider crop variety, quality, transport costs, fees and local demand. Rate-GPT displays reported observations; it does not set prices, predict future rates, certify a buyer or replace advice from your local mandi.
