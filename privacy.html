Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Rate-GPT handles when you browse the website or contact the team.

Information we handle

Rate-GPT does not require account registration. The site stores limited preferences, such as language and recently selected mandi, in this browser’s local storage on this device. These preferences are not uploaded to Rate-GPT. Data saved by retired local tools may remain in this browser until you clear this site’s storage; the current site does not read or use that data. If you use voice crop input, microphone access starts only after you tap the voice button; Rate-GPT does not record or store audio, but your browser may process speech under its own privacy settings. If you use the Weather page, your searched place, selected coordinates or opt-in approximate IP location are sent directly to WeatherAPI to return weather information. If you send the contact form, the details you enter include your name, message and at least one contact method (email address or phone number). The contact form sends the submitted fields directly from your browser to Web3Forms over HTTPS. Rate-GPT’s application server does not receive or store the contact message in this browser-side flow. Web3Forms processes the submission and delivers it to the project team. Do not include passwords, OTPs, payment details, identity documents or sensitive personal information. The service worker caches static website files for offline fallback; it does not cache /api responses. Web hosting and delivery providers may process technical request data needed to operate and secure their services; consult their own privacy notices for their practices.

How information is used

Contact details are used to read and answer your request, investigate reported website issues, and prevent abuse. They are not requested for advertising. Access should be limited to people handling support and to service providers needed to deliver the form.

Government data and external services

Mandi records are requested from the Government of India Open Government Data platform (data.gov.in) / AGMARKNET when a user searches and may be requested again automatically while the mandi page remains open. The source controls when records are published; a fresh fetch time does not mean a new market observation or a real-time trading price. The site also uses WeatherAPI for weather searches and forecasts, Vercel for hosting, Web3Forms for contact-message delivery, Google Fonts for typefaces, and Microsoft Clarity for website usage insights. WeatherAPI receives the location query needed to return weather data. When Clarity is active, Microsoft may process information such as pages visited, browser/device details, and interactions with page elements to provide aggregated usage insights and session-replay features. Review Microsoft's privacy information for its processing and retention practices.

Advertising and third-party cookies

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on Rate-GPT, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to collect information as a result of displaying ads. These technologies may be used to serve, measure, limit or personalise advertising, depending on the user's settings, location and the applicable service rules. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies in a browser or use web beacons to collect information in connection with ads shown on this website. Rate-GPT does not ask visitors to click ads and does not receive a benefit from accidental clicks. Advertising is not currently represented as active unless ads are actually enabled on the site.

For information about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, read Google's partner-site privacy explanation. You can review and manage advertising personalisation through your Google account settings where available. Browser settings may also let you control or delete cookies; blocking cookies can affect some website or advertising features.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the contact form and may clear your browser site data to remove locally stored preferences. To ask a question about a submitted message or request access, correction or deletion where applicable, use the Contact Us form and identify the request as a privacy request. We will review it and coordinate with the relevant service provider where possible. Provider retention, backups and legal obligations may affect deletion timing. We do not promise a fixed retention period until the provider’s account settings and retention terms have been verified.

Privacy requests and safeguards

Rate-GPT aims to collect only the details needed for a contact request, transmit the Web3Forms credential from the server rather than the browser, and use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Do not send information about another person unless you are authorised to do so. For privacy questions or requests, use Contact Us and write “Privacy request” in your message. This policy is reviewed against applicable Indian data-protection requirements as their commencement provisions take effect.
