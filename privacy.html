Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Rate-GPT handles when you browse the website or contact the team.

Information we handle

We do not require account registration. The site may store basic preferences such as your selected language or recently selected mandi in your browser’s local storage. If you submit the contact form, the name, message and the email address and/or phone number you provide are sent to Web3Forms for delivery to the project team. Web3Forms may process technical information needed to provide its service; review its own privacy policy for details.

How information is used

Contact details are used only to review and respond to the request, investigate reported issues and protect the service from abuse. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment information, identity documents or sensitive personal information.

Government data and external services

Mandi records are obtained from public government data sources, including the Government of India Open Government Data platform (data.gov.in) and its AGMARKNET dataset. Updates depend on source publication and successful synchronization. External websites and services have their own policies and availability.

Your choices

You may avoid using the contact form, clear browser site data to remove locally stored preferences, or contact us to request that a submitted message be reviewed. Deletion from third-party service systems is subject to that provider’s processes and legal obligations.

Privacy requests and safeguards

Rate-GPT aims to collect only the details needed for a contact request, transmit the Web3Forms credential from the server rather than the browser, and use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Do not send information about another person unless you are authorised to do so. For privacy questions or requests, use Contact Us and write “Privacy request” in your message. This policy is reviewed against applicable Indian data-protection requirements as their commencement provisions take effect.
