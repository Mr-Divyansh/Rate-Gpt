Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Rate-GPT handles when you browse the website or contact the team.

Information we handle

Rate-GPT does not require account registration. The site stores limited preferences, such as language and recently selected mandi, in your browser local storage. If you send the contact form, Rate-GPT collects your name, message and at least one contact method (email address or phone number). The form is sent to Rate-GPT’s server and then to Web3Forms, which processes the submission to deliver it to the project team. Do not include passwords, OTPs, payment details, identity documents or sensitive personal information. Web hosting and delivery providers may process technical request data needed to operate and secure their services; consult their own privacy notices for their practices.

How information is used

Contact details are used to read and answer your request, investigate reported website issues, and prevent abuse. They are not requested for advertising. Access should be limited to people handling support and to service providers needed to deliver the form.

Government data and external services

Mandi records are requested from the Government of India Open Government Data platform (data.gov.in) / AGMARKNET when a user searches and may be requested again automatically while the mandi page remains open. The source controls when records are published; a fresh fetch time does not mean a new market observation or a real-time trading price. The site also uses Vercel for hosting, Web3Forms for contact-message delivery, and Google Fonts for typefaces. Those providers may process technical data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the contact form and may clear your browser site data to remove locally stored preferences. To ask a question about a submitted message or request access, correction or deletion where applicable, use the Contact Us form and identify the request as a privacy request. We will review it and coordinate with the relevant service provider where possible. Provider retention, backups and legal obligations may affect deletion timing. We do not promise a fixed retention period until the provider’s account settings and retention terms have been verified.

Privacy requests and safeguards

Rate-GPT aims to collect only the details needed for a contact request, transmit the Web3Forms credential from the server rather than the browser, and use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Do not send information about another person unless you are authorised to do so. For privacy questions or requests, use Contact Us and write “Privacy request” in your message. This policy is reviewed against applicable Indian data-protection requirements as their commencement provisions take effect.
