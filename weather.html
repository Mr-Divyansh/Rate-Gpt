    A practical guide to local forecasts

    How to read the weather information on Rate-GPT

    Use the selected place and the time of the observation before interpreting a temperature, condition or forecast. Weather can vary between nearby villages, valleys and higher locations, so a result for one place may not describe the conditions across an entire district.

    Choose the closest matching place

    Search by village, town, district or PIN code, then select the matching suggestion. Check the displayed place name and region before relying on the result. If the exact village is unavailable, a nearby town is only an approximation—not a measurement from your field.

    Understand current conditions and forecasts

    Current conditions describe the provider’s latest available observation for the selected location. The three-day outlook is a forecast, not a promise. Forecast details can change as new observations and model updates become available.

    Read the measurements in context

    Temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, pressure and UV are different measurements. Wind is shown in kilometres per hour and pressure in millibars. A single measurement does not describe every local condition or determine whether it is safe to work outdoors.

    Use forecasts carefully for farm decisions

    Do not use a general forecast as the only basis for irrigation, spraying, harvesting, travel or other weather-sensitive work. Check the latest local conditions and official warnings from the India Meteorological Department, and follow guidance from qualified local agricultural or disaster-management authorities when relevant.

    Weather data provider: WeatherAPI. Rate-GPT displays third-party information and does not operate weather stations or issue official warnings.
